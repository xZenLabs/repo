# koreader · 2026-11-19

DogEarBooks 1.5 builds on KitanaCode’s Book Nook Charms with a visual gallery, folder browsing, and new polar bear and cinema-themed bookmark collections.

Choose favorites by holding their previews, adjust size and position in Charm Studio, and enter position values directly with the keyboard. Your adjustments stay in place when you switch images. Day and night variants are included for the new collections, with automatic switching available.

This release also improves gallery loading and touch handling, adds translations, and draws bookmarks above reading overlays such as Bookends.
