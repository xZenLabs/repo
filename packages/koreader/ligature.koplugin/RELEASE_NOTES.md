# v1.0.0 · 2026-10-05

Swipe typing for KOReader, tuned for e-ink and one-handed use. Learns your words and where your thumb lands.

First release as Ligature, which supersedes Tapless 0.4.0.

- far more swipes recognised: long words by the shape of the whole swipe, swipes that start on the next key, cut corners and double letters
- learns the words you use, word pairs for each language and where your thumb lands
- word completions while tapping
- one-handed keyboard that moves, resizes and remembers its place
- languages picked on first run: English comes with the plugin, the other 10 are downloaded
- thin grey swipe trail that also shows in night mode
- hold a suggestion to block it; offensive words ranked down
- contractions typed with their apostrophes
- backspace after a swipe deletes the whole word; slide left to delete more
- a second finger no longer cuts a swipe short

### Installing

Unzip `ligature.koplugin.zip`, copy the `ligature.koplugin` folder into KOReader's `plugins` folder (`koreader/plugins` on a Kindle over USB) and restart KOReader.
