# v1.0.2 · 2026-11-17


Phones and small screens, and an easier way to choose the library's folder.

### Added

- **The library's top folder** can be changed from inside SolarUI: a Top folder button in the "Library opens in" picker, and Settings, Home, Library opens in, Change the library's top folder.

### Fixed

- **Phones and other narrow, sharp screens:** text no longer runs over or gets cut off; it now fits the layout, on home and everywhere else.
- **A crowded dock** takes two rows when there is room above it, instead of squeezing every button into one.
- The folder picker no longer looks empty without explaining why when the library's top folder has no folders in it.

# v1.0.1 · 2026-11-16


A quick fix for 1.0.0.

### Fixed

- Home now shows the book progress style chosen in Settings (vine, blossoms or moons); it always showed the vine.

# v1.0.0 · 2026-11-16


The first release: an Art Nouveau garden of a home screen for KOReader.

### Added

- **Home:** your book in a halo of roses, a progress vine, your reading as flowers, a shelf of recent books and a dock of plants.
- **The garden:** plants to water and animals to feed with the dew your pages earn, seeds from finished books, visitors earned by your reading, four seasons, weather and night animals.
- **Companions:** 32 plants and animals, each with a voice of their own and nearly 10,000 lines between them, opening new lines as your bond grows.
- **Outdoors:** three small outdoor tasks each week, with a log.
- **The library, collections, reading log, goal tree and book details** in the garden's style, and Ink Away's notebooks on a shelf of their own.
- **KOReader's menu and windows** in SolarUI's style, five colour schemes with light and dark modes, font sets and a layout editor.
- **A twin:** one tap switches home to CyberUI and back.
- **The opening card:** while a book opens, where you resume, the time left and a line you once marked in it.
- **Book progress** as a vine, blossoms or moons.
- Ten languages besides English.
