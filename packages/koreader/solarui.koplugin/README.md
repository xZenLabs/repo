SolarUI is an **Art Nouveau garden of a home screen** for KOReader. Read, and the garden grows.

## What grows here

* **32 companions, nearly 10,000 lines.** Any of the 19 plants and 13 animals can keep you company on home. Each has a voice of their own: they greet you, answer a tap, notice today's reading and share memories as you read. New lines open as your bond grows over five levels.
* **A garden your reading grows.** Every page you read is dew to water plants and feed animals, and every finished book is a seed. A vine climbs the garden's frame, a leaf for each day you read and a flower every thirty; the maple turns a leaf gold for each book this year. Morning reading, night reading and streaks bring new visitors, from the fox to the peacock.
* **Alive on its own clock.** Four seasons from your calendar, weather, and night animals that come out only in the dark.
* **A nudge to go outside.** Each week the garden sets three small outdoor tasks: something to look at, something to listen to, something to do. Tick them off and your companion notices; a log keeps every week you went out.
* **Your highlights on the garden table**, field notes on everyone you meet, and an apple tree for the year's reading goal.
* **A twin.** One tap turns home into [CyberUI](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/CyberUI.koplugin), and back.

## Install

<a href="https://github.com/EmirErtorer/SolarUI.koplugin/releases/latest"><img src="assets/readme-download.svg" alt="Download the latest release"></a>

Unzip the download and copy the `SolarUI.koplugin` folder into KOReader's `plugins` directory, then restart KOReader:

- Kindle: `koreader/plugins/`
- Kobo: `.adds/koreader/plugins/`
- Android: `koreader/plugins/` in app storage

Then open the top menu, the first tab (the file browser's), **Start with**, **SolarUI**.

## Notes

- **Twin of [CyberUI](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/CyberUI.koplugin).** Install both and switch between them with one tap; each keeps its own settings.
- **In eleven languages:** English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian and European Portuguese, Russian, Polish and Greek, following KOReader's language. The companions speak English for now.
- **Works with [Ink Away](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin)** for notebooks, drawings and writing on books.

<details>
<summary>Development</summary>

- `tests/run.sh` runs the lint, luacheck and the headless test suites on
  KOReader's real drawing code. It needs a KOReader emulator build (set `KO_DIR`
  if it is not in `~/koreader-emulator`). Add `--e2e` to also run the scenarios
  in the visible emulator.
- `tools/emulator.sh` copies the plugin into the emulator build and starts it.
- `tools/layout-editor.sh` renders the home and garden screens in the emulator
  at six screen sizes and opens a page where every named part (`home.cover`,
  `garden.btn_season`...) can be dragged, stretched or hidden, or changed by
  typing its name. The result is saved in `solarui/data/layout_overrides.lua`,
  in the poster's units, so one tweak fits every screen size. Parts are named
  in code with `scene:group(id, label, add)`.

- `tools/readme/shots.sh` takes the screenshots above and the colour scheme collage, `tools/readme/gif.sh <companions|garden|twin> <out.gif>` makes the moving pictures, and `tools/readme/header.lua` and `tools/readme/banners.py` draw the header and the button.

</details>
