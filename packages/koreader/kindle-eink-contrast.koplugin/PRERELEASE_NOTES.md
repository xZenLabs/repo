# v0.3.3-preview · 2026-11-19

## What's new

- Added the 20°C preset alongside 15°C, 10°C, 5°C, and 0°C.
- Added Kindle Scribe (2022 and 2024) support. The contributor tested on a 2024 Scribe.
- Included the detected framebuffer driver in unexpected-driver logs.

Thanks to mlbrnm for [PR #1](https://github.com/mkdayyyy/kindle-eink-contrast/pull/1).

## Install

Download the plugin ZIP under **Assets**. Exit KOReader, extract the ZIP, and copy `kindle-eink-contrast.koplugin` to `koreader/plugins/`. Reopen KOReader, open a book, and find **Kindle E Ink Contrast** under **Settings > Screen**. Use **Restore Auto** to return to the default.

# v0.3.2-preview · 2026-11-19

Download the plugin ZIP under **Assets** and extract it. With KOReader closed, copy the extracted `kindle-eink-contrast.koplugin` folder into `koreader/plugins/` on your Kindle.

Open a book in KOReader and go to **Settings → Screen → Kindle E Ink Contrast** to choose a preset. Use **Restore Auto** to restore the default setting.

The README shows before-and-after photos from a Paperwhite 5. Results can vary by Kindle model and firmware.
