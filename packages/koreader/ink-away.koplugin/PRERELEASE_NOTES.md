# v2.2.2

Diagnostic build for palm rejection on pen devices (Kindle Scribe, Kobo with a stylus). Turn on Settings > Guides and aids > Palm rejection (pen), then draw with the pen. You should see a note that the pen hook is registered, and a one-time 'pen detected' popup the first time the pen touches. Please report whether each note appears (a photo of the 'pen detected' popup is ideal). If the pen still behaves like a finger and no 'pen detected' popup appears, your device isn't handing the pen to plugins. Needs KOReader 2026.07 or newer.
