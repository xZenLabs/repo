# v1.3.0 · 2026-11-09

A big update: a new look, murdoku.com-style controls, and a brand-new Originals pack.

### New look
- Suspect cards under the board (beside it in landscape) with faces, the name with its initial as a badge, and clue keywords in **bold**.
- Object icons on the board: obstacles are dark squares with white icons, things you can stand, sit or lie on are light.
- Rows and columns labelled R1… / C1…, an icon toolbar, and a polished case list.
- **Clue highlighting**: select a suspect to outline the squares their clue allows (areas, "beside", rows and columns, corners, "not …", "X or Y", and directions relative to people you've placed). You can turn it off in the menu.

### Controls
- Tap a square for a note, hold to place, as on murdoku.com. Placing someone crosses out their row and column; taking them off clears only those automatic X marks.
- Eraser tool, unlimited undo, and hold any square or suspect for details ("You can stand here." / "You can't stand here.").

### Cases
- A new **Originals** pack: 30 cases (very easy to hard, 5×5 to 9×9), each built around a satisfying "aha" step, with one-idea-at-a-time hints.
- Random cases use the same generator for very easy and easy boards up to 7×7.
- Saved progress on the old Originals doesn't carry over, because those cases have been replaced.

### Fixes
- The timer no longer counts while the device sleeps.
- How to play rewritten, with an example for every kind of clue.
- Long area names shrink or wrap instead of being cut off; notes no longer hide under area names.

Install with [Storefront](https://github.com/ultimatejimmy/storefront.koplugin) or download `murdoku.koplugin.zip` below and copy the `murdoku.koplugin` folder into `koreader/plugins/`.
