# experimental_v1.0 · 2026-10-12

Experimental option: Change pattern on page turn.

When enabled, every style's texture shifts slightly each time you turn the page, instead of staying frozen in the same spot. The pattern itself isn't regenerated; the same dots, fibers, or lines are just redrawn at a new position, so there's no extra cost beyond a page turn already has.
