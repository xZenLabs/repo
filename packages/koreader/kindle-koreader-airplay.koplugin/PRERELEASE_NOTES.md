# v1.4.0-rc.12 · 2026-11-11

## Install

1. Download the zip matching your Kindle's firmware:
   - `airplay.koplugin-sf-v1.4.0-rc.12.zip` — firmware <=5.16.2.1.1 (Paperwhite 1-5, Voyage, Oasis 1-3)
   - `airplay.koplugin-hf-v1.4.0-rc.12.zip` — firmware >=5.16.3 (Paperwhite 6/12, current Scribe, or any device updated past 5.16.2.1.1)
2. Unzip and copy `airplay.koplugin/` to your Kindle:
   ```
   cp -r airplay.koplugin /Volumes/Kindle/extensions/koreader/plugins/
   ```
3. Restart KOReader

**Not sure which one you need?** Check Settings → Device Info on the Kindle, or `cat /etc/prettyversion.txt` over SSH — anything 5.16.3 or newer is the hard-float (hf) build.

## Illusion app (separate package)

Download `airplay-illusion-sf-v1.4.0-rc.12.zip` for firmware <=5.16.2.1.1 or `airplay-illusion-hf-v1.4.0-rc.12.zip` for firmware >=5.16.3. Extract `illusion-airplay.sh` and the `illusion-airplay/` folder into `/mnt/us/documents/`, then tap **Kindle AirPlay** in the Kindle library. It opens a clean AirPlay screen and switches to the mirrored picture after connection. Keep the Kindle awake while receiving.

See [README](https://github.com/5kxh2dxqmd-afk/kindle-koreader-airplay/blob/main/README.md) for full setup instructions.


**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/5kxh2dxqmd-afk/kindle-koreader-airplay/compare/v1.4.0-rc.11...v1.4.0-rc.12
