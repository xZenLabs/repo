# v1.2.0 · 2026-11-01

- Play the Crystal story through Goldenrod City: catch Pokémon, build a party of six and take on Falkner, Bugsy and Whitney. Open **Explore → Story** to start.
- Add your partner to SimpleUI with live stats, short animations and optional Tiny Critter images.
- Simpler battles, without guarding or charging, and fixes for reading scans on non-Kindle KOReader devices.

Back up your whole `pokepal` folder before updating. Story saves use version 13, which older builds cannot read. PokePal keeps a **Before story upgrade** backup in **More → Backups**. Restore it in this build before downgrading, and update both editions if you use KOReader and KUAL.

The story ends after Whitney’s Plain Badge. Physical Kindle touch, e-ink refresh and power-button/cover sleep and wake still need testing.

[Story guide and limits](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/blob/v1.2.0/docs/crystal-story.md) · [SimpleUI setup](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/blob/v1.2.0/docs/simpleui-tiny-critter.md)

# v1.1.0 · 2026-10-29

- Explore Fern Town and four wild areas, recruit Pokémon with berries, build a party of up to six and store your collection in the PC box. Challenge Gym leaders, fresh trainer teams and the Battle Tower.
- New Crystal maps and trainer artwork, front and back battle sprites, and walking animations in all four directions for all 386 Pokémon.
- Major bug fixes include overlapping town messages, messages lingering on other screens, resting partners getting stuck after battles, and missing home backgrounds after waking.

With [Bookshelf](https://github.com/AndyHazz/bookshelf.koplugin) 6.0 or later, your partner can sit on the KOReader home screen. Add **PokePal** in the layout editor and tap it to play. Thanks to [AndyHazz](https://github.com/AndyHazz) for the module.

Back up your `pokepal` folder before updating. Older versions cannot read saves upgraded by Explore. Update both editions if you use both.

Includes [PR #9](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/pull/9), [PR #10](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/pull/10) and [PR #11](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/pull/11). [Source download](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/raw/refs/heads/release-sources/v1.1.0/PokePal-v1.1.0-corresponding-source.zip).

# v1.0.9 · 2026-10-26

All 386 Pokémon from Kanto, Johto and Hoenn are now available.

- Browse by region and use every evolution branch through Gen 3.
- Open PokePal directly from Tools in KOReader, with a one-time welcome and shortcut help. Open PokePal is also available for gestures and QuickMenu.
- Scrolling now counts towards reading rewards, eggs and expeditions. Reading pauses while the sleep screen, welcome or shortcut help is open.
- Fixed search, nickname and book-title dialogs appearing behind the game.
- Fixed the sleeping-partner image and touch controls after waking in landscape. Removed unnecessary game saves when automatic rest is off.
- Added the missing Dark-type journal filter.

Includes [PR #7](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/pull/7) and [PR #8](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/pull/8).

Download the KUAL or KOReader ZIP below. Back up your `pokepal` folder before updating, update both editions if you use both, and restart KOReader after copying the files.

# v1.0.7 · 2026-10-23

Pick any of the 160 Pokémon and switch from **Profile > Change companion**. Each keeps its XP, bond and nickname.

- Fixed oversized text and overflowing buttons in KOReader.
- Added journal search and favourites, book memories, trail choices and eight new badges.
- Optional auto-rest, a KOReader sleep screen, larger text and stronger contrast.
- Less startup waiting, fewer KUAL screen flashes and fewer unnecessary save writes.

Download the **KUAL** or **KOReader** ZIP below. Back up your saves and update both editions if you use both. Older builds cannot read the new saves.
