# v1.1.0 · 2026-10-29

- Explore Fern Town and four wild areas, recruit Pokémon with berries, build a party of up to six and store your collection in the PC box. Challenge Gym leaders, fresh trainer teams and the Battle Tower.
- New Crystal maps and trainer artwork, front and back battle sprites, and walking animations in all four directions for all 386 Pokémon.
- Major bug fixes include overlapping town messages, messages lingering on other screens, resting partners getting stuck after battles, and missing home backgrounds after waking.

With [Bookshelf](https://github.com/AndyHazz/bookshelf.koplugin) 6.0 or later, your partner can sit on the KOReader home screen. Add **PokePal** in the layout editor and tap it to play. Thanks to [AndyHazz](https://github.com/AndyHazz) for the module.

Back up your `pokepal` folder before updating. Older versions cannot read saves upgraded by Explore. Update both editions if you use both.

# v1.0.6 · 2026-10-22

Home keeps your Pokémon's care stats, bond and XP. Reading rewards stay under More, and trips default to a timer.

- Six backgrounds, with cushions, plants, lamps and bookshelves to buy with berries.
- Nicknames and reactions that change with your partner's mood and bond.
- Berry patches, footprints and campsites along expedition routes.
- Eggs that crack and hatch as you read, plus optional weekly goals and a reading recap under More.
- Clearer XP and bond bars on the profile, and backup controls with a preview before restoring.

Fixed stale expedition and weekly progress after leaving the app open, disappearing save warnings and the animation that could show an away Pokémon at home.

### KUAL

Needs a jailbroken touchscreen Kindle with KUAL installed. KOReader is not required.

1. Unzip **PokePal-v1.0.6-kual.zip**.
2. Copy `extensions` onto the Kindle drive, next to `documents`. Merge folders if asked.
3. Eject, then open **KUAL > PokePal > Play PokePal**.

### KOReader

1. Unzip **PokePal-v1.0.6-koreader.zip**.
2. Merge its `koreader` folder with the one on your Kindle.
3. Restart KOReader, then open **Tools > More tools > PokePal**.

Back up the `pokepal` folder before updating. If you use both editions, update both. Older builds cannot read saves with these new features.

Reading rewards are optional. Kindle tracking depends on the timing data saved with each book. [How tracking works](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/blob/v1.0.6/docs/reading-rewards.md).
