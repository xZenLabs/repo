## Sharing

**Share**, on the summary card, shows a QR code and an address. On the same Wi-Fi network, point your phone at the code or type the address in any browser, tap **Allow** on the e-reader, and your recap opens in the browser, with nothing to install.

- **Choose a style.** Vibrant, e-paper or black and white, with backgrounds that move.
- **Save one or all.** Images come in story size, 1080x1920, ready to post, one at a time or all together in a zip file.

> [!WARNING]
> The connection is plain HTTP, because a local server has no certificate to offer. Someone capturing traffic on the same network could see your recap. Use your home Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot, not public networks.

<details>
<summary><b>What protects your device</b></summary>

<br>

- **Only on while in use.** The server exists while the screen is open, and it stops on Stop, on sleep and after 10 minutes without use.
- **A code in the address**, random and new every time. Without it, every answer is the same "not found".
- **Approval on the device.** The first device with the code needs your tap, and after that no other address is served.
- **Private networks only.** It refuses to start outside 10.x, 172.16-31.x and 192.168.x, and refuses clients from outside.
- **Read only.** It answers `GET` and nothing else, so nothing can be sent to your device. No file path can be asked for: only the cards of the year on the screen.

The full list is in [docs/design.md](docs/design.md).

</details>

## Installation

1. Download `book_rewind.koplugin.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/davydmaker/book_rewind.koplugin/releases/latest).
2. Extract it into `koreader/plugins/`, so that `koreader/plugins/book_rewind.koplugin/main.lua` exists.
3. Restart KOReader. The plugin is in the Tools menu, as **Book Rewind**.

The language follows KOReader's: English, Portuguese or Spanish. In any other language, it uses English.

> [!TIP]
> A year needs at least 10 minutes of reading to get its cards. In the first 30 days of a new year, the plugin opens on the year that just ended.

## Your data stays with you

> [!NOTE]
> Book Rewind only reads KOReader's statistics (`statistics.sqlite3`), the library's cover cache (`bookinfo_cache.sqlite3`) and, for page counts, the settings KOReader keeps for each book you opened this year, all read-only. It makes no internet connections. Nothing leaves your device unless you tap **Share**, and then only to the device you allow on your own network.

<details>
<summary><b>How the numbers work</b></summary>

<br>

- **It only counts what went through KOReader.** Reading in other apps is not included.
- **Title and author** come from the file. Without a title, it shows the file name. Without an author, the book stays out of the authors card.
- **Read to the end** means 90% of the book read in KOReader. It counts in the year that mark is reached, even for a book started years before.
- **Books marked as finished** in KOReader also count as read to the end when the statistics don't show them, for example after you reset the statistics: the book needs to be marked as finished in the year, with at least 90% read, and be in KOReader's reading history. They count on the books card, the timeline (in the month they were marked) and the summary, but not in time, pages or the other cards, since their reading sessions are gone.
- **Pages** count the part of the book you covered, so rereading a passage at another font size does not add up twice.
- **Stable page numbers:** in a book where you turned on KOReader's stable page numbers (publisher pages or characters per page), pages count in them. Other books count screen pages, as KOReader shows them. This needs the book in KOReader's reading history.
- **Covers** come from KOReader's library. Books with no cached cover get one generated on the spot, with the title and author.

The complete rules, including the thresholds of each profile, are in [docs/design.md](docs/design.md).

</details>

## Compatibility

- Tested with KOReader 2026.07 on a 10th generation basic Kindle (firmware 5.18.1).
- Layout checked in the emulator at 600x800, 758x1024, 1072x1448 and 1264x1680.
- Made for touchscreens. On devices with keys, the page keys navigate and the back key closes, but changing the year and sharing need touch.
- Always in portrait. Opened in landscape, it rotates the screen and restores the rotation on exit.
- Each card flashes the screen once. If you still see marks from the previous card, the checkbox on the cover makes it flash twice.
- If the device sleeps with Book Rewind open, it shows the Book Rewind cover instead of the book cover. This applies when the KOReader sleep screen is set to show the book cover.
