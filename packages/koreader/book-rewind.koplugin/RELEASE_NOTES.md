# v1.4.0 · 2026-11-10


- In books where you turned on KOReader's stable page numbers, pages count in them: the year, the biggest day, the devoured book, the pace and the other cards with pages. Other books keep counting screen pages.

# v1.3.0 · 2026-11-06


- **Save all** on the share page saves every card together in a zip file.
- Every card now redraws the whole screen with a flash, to reduce the ghost of the previous card.
- A checkbox on the cover flashes the screen twice on each card, for screens where one flash still leaves marks.
- On color e-ink, cards use the color modes of the screen, the same KOReader uses for covers.
- Texts on the share page and screen say "e-reader" instead of "Kindle".
- On the summary card, "Author" and "Month" are now "Top author" and "Best month".

# v1.2.0 · 2026-11-06


- Three themes: gray for black and white screens, a palette tuned for color e-ink, and a vibrant one for phones. Your device picks its own.
- Every card now has its own background, with shapes that fit its subject: stars, clouds, pages, hills. All themes share the same art.
- The hours card changes with your favorite time of day: morning and afternoon have the sun instead of the moon.
- **Share** replaces **Save Images**. It shows a QR code, and a browser on the same Wi-Fi network gets your recap, with moving backgrounds and a choice of theme. Saved images come in story size, 1080x1920. Nothing is stored on the e-reader anymore, and images saved by older versions stay where they are.
- New drawings for book spines and generated covers. The summary card carries a Book Rewind seal, and the warranty line on the About card is clearer.
- Fixed the sand of the hourglass, which crossed the glass, and the checkered flag of the Marathoner, which now has true squares.

# v1.1.1 · 2026-11-05


- A book opened again after it was finished keeps the day it reached the end. Before, looking something up months later moved it in the timeline, made the devoured book take longer, and could create a slow burn or a book that waited.
- A book read in a single day and opened again later can now be the book read in one day.
- The pages of your biggest day and the page where a book stopped follow the same rules as the rest of the cards, even after a change of font size.
- The reread card counts readings to the end, so turning pages back and forth no longer adds readings.
- Generated covers no longer print labels such as "Windows User" as the author, and a title shared by different books no longer brings the cover of another book.
- If Book Rewind cannot open, a notice appears and the screen rotation is restored. A card that cannot be drawn shows a message, and the others stay available.
- Saving images removes the images of that year left by an older save with more cards.
- Covers load faster in large libraries, and opening a year uses less memory.
