# v1.4.2 · 2026-11-12


- Covers of PDFs, CBZs and other books without a title in their metadata now show up, found by file name. Before, those books got a generated cover.

# v1.4.1 · 2026-11-10


- Books marked as finished in KOReader count as read to the end when the statistics don't show them, for example after a reset: on the books card, the timeline and the summary. They need at least 90% read and to be in KOReader's reading history.

# v1.4.0 · 2026-11-10


- In books where you turned on KOReader's stable page numbers, pages count in them: the year, the biggest day, the devoured book, the pace and the other cards with pages. Other books keep counting screen pages.

# v1.3.1 · 2026-11-08


- The Book Rewind cover on the sleep screen while the plugin is open.

# v1.3.0 · 2026-11-06


- **Save all** on the share page saves every card together in a zip file.
- Every card now redraws the whole screen with a flash, to reduce the ghost of the previous card.
- A checkbox on the cover flashes the screen twice on each card, for screens where one flash still leaves marks.
- On color e-ink, cards use the color modes of the screen, the same KOReader uses for covers.
- Texts on the share page and screen say "e-reader" instead of "Kindle".
- On the summary card, "Author" and "Month" are now "Top author" and "Best month".
