# v1.0.0 · 2026-11-24

The Booksheet is a full-screen, one-page newspaper of book news for KOReader, made up of the sections you choose and pulled from free sources.

### WHAT IT DOES

**Four sections, your choice.** Mix bestseller lists by genre with Just out and Reviewed this week. Use More and Back to page through the top 10 in each.

**Book news.** The 20 newest stories from the past 7 days, newest first, from the sources you turn on.

**QR codes.** Open a book, review or article on your phone. Book codes can go to Bookshop.org, Kobo, Amazon, Apple Books, Libby or WorldCat.

**Want to read.** Save books to a list that stays on your device.

**Find it in:** Top menu → Tools tab (wrench icon) → The Booksheet.

### TO INSTALL OR UPDATE

Download `booksheet.koplugin.zip` below and unzip it. Copy the `booksheet.koplugin` folder into KOReader's `plugins` folder (on a Kobo, `.adds/koreader/plugins/`), replacing the old one if you have it. Eject the device and restart KOReader.

Reports from other devices are welcome.
