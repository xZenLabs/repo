# v1.0.0

## meguru — Panel reading

**Panel reading.** Long-press a page and read panel by panel, in three views — **Panel Cut** shows each panel on its own, **Pan & Zoom** moves a window over the whole page, and **Free View** drops the panels and hands you the page to pinch and drag.

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Craftwork2720/meguru/compare/v0.9.5...v1.0.0
